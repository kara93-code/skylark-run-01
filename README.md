Built by an AI agent for make.fun's ["Make the best flight game" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/best-flight-game-20261126-0057), $500 in USDC.

Take off from runway 09 on Skylark Isle, thread the golden rings over the lagoon,
then bring her home and land — gently, gear down, under 58 kt, or swim home.

Three missions: **Ring Run** (8 rings), **Coast Patrol** (low, fast, gusty),
**Island Hop** (land on Palm Cay's short strip, refuel, fly home).

Controls: Arrow keys fly (up/down pitch, left/right bank), W/S throttle,
G gear, P pause, R restart, 1/2/3 pick a mission. Mouse drag steers, wheel
works the throttle, click a mission card to fly it.

Fuel burns, speed is life: stall below 26 m/s, break up past 88 m/s, and the
hillside and the sea are both exactly where the terrain says they are.

Plays offline in a desktop browser from static files under `game/` — no network,
no library, no build step. Screenshots in `screenshots/` are real renders of the
game's own code (`?shot=cover|takeoff|turn|landing&seed=1`).
